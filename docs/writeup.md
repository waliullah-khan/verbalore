# Verbalore: write-up

**Problem.** Founders, sales leaders and agents all know video works, and most of them still post nothing. Making one video means finding something worth saying, writing it, getting through it on camera, then editing it. That is a lot of work for one clip, so it gets skipped. In outbound the same problem is worse: a funding round or a new VP is the best moment to reach someone, and a generic email sent a week later misses it.

**Who it helps.** Anyone who sells or builds in public: real estate agents, developers, product marketers, GTM and C-suite leaders, and founders. The account-based side is for revenue teams who already keep their pipeline in a CRM.

**Solution.** Verbalore removes every step except being on camera. Each morning it researches what people in your niche are posting across seven networks, scores posts against others on the same platform, and emails five ideas with a real reference example and a script in your voice. You open one, read from the teleprompter, and stop. A worker cuts the transcript into scenes, gives every scene a graphic, and renders a finished vertical video. One screen posts it to up to 15 platforms. For account-based marketing, a signal engine searches for funding, leadership, launch and hiring changes at each CRM account, ranks them, and drafts a 30 second video note to the right contact, delivered as a share page that records views.

**Impact.** A 45 second video renders in roughly two and a quarter to three minutes on a laptop, and the research and scripting steps run before you sit down. Everything else in the loop is a click. Every provider has a labelled fallback, so the whole flow can be demonstrated live, and every number in the README was measured on the project's own code.
